A LITTLE CONTEXT FOR YOUR JOURNEY

A starting point.
An open world.

UNISEEK is an independent university discovery project by Jose Zhang Haozhe. It brings together locations and scores for 50 universities in each of six ranking categories, so you can explore, save, and compare your possibilities.

A snapshot, not today’s rankings. This project uses its original QS 2021 dataset. Scores are displayed as recorded and are best compared within a subject area. For current rankings and methodology, visit QS Top Universities ↗.
